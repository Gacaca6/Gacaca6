### 🚀 What I'm building

I ship products fast, with a focus on **AI for African impact**. My work clusters around a few missions:

- 🎓 **Education** — making learning free, local, and accessible (`Learn4Africa`, `Kina-Wige`, `FinEd-Africa`)
- 💰 **Financial inclusion** — helping young Africans save, invest, and manage money (`Ireme-Youth`, `Bk-Capital-Tracker`, `Kigali-Budget-Plan`, `RRA-Assistant`)
- 🧠 **Health & wellbeing** — youth mental health and digital discipline (`NuruMind`)
- 💼 **Opportunity** — connecting rural youth to jobs and gigs (`AkaziConnect`)
- 🎨 **Creative AI** — voice, art, and music tools (`Gicanda-AI`, `Yuhi-Artify-Studio`, `MusiMe`)
